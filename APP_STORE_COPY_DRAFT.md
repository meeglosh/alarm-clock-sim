## Description

Let's not pretend. This is AI slop, top to bottom — the art, the music, all of it. We made a game where an alarm goes off every 10 minutes and you have to hit snooze before it stops, forever, and we're not sorry.

Here's the thing: it's kind of great anyway.

HOW TO PLAY
• The alarm rings every 10 minutes. Snooze it in time, your streak grows.
• Miss the window, you oversleep, it's over.
• Had enough? Hold the hammer down and smash the clock. Dramatic. Final. Deeply satisfying.
• A new mission generates every single day, forever, whether you want one or not.
• Unlock a museum of clocks you've personally destroyed.
• Climb a real global leaderboard against other people who also can't stop doing this.

IS IT PAY TO WIN?
Yes. You can buy your way out of the suffering with a streak freeze, or go full unlimited with a subscription so the alarm can never end your run — even by accident. We're not going to pretend that's not what this is.
